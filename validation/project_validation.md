# Scoring Framework

## Reality Alignment (RA)

Score: 0–10

Measures alignment between project assumptions and observable reality.

## Capability

Score: 0–10

Measures execution capacity and resource availability.

## Freedom

Score: 0–10

Measures available options and flexibility.

## Friction

Score: 0–10

Measures obstacles, constraints, and resistance.

## RVSS

Score: 0–10

Measures future viability and expansion potential.
